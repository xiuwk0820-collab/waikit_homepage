About Me

I am Waikit Xiu, currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at The University of Hong Kong after receiving my B.Eng. in Traffic Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University. My research focuses on reasoning for autonomous driving and embodied intelligence, especially in dynamic and safety-critical real-world environments.

More specifically, I am interested in building intelligent systems that understand how the world evolves over time. My recent work spans video spatio-temporal modeling, vision-language large models, and world models for real-world autonomous driving and embodied settings.

My current research interests include:

  • Video Spatio-temporal Modeling: learning structured representations from video streams to capture scene dynamics, temporal dependencies, and evolving interactions in complex real-world environments.
  • Vision-Language Large Models: leveraging multimodal large language models for cross-modal understanding, retrieval-augmented reasoning, and interpretable scene-level intelligence beyond perception alone.
  • World Models: building internal models of environment dynamics and agent behavior to support spatio-temporal reasoning, planning, and decision-making in autonomous driving and embodied AI.

I am actively seeking research-oriented RA and Ph.D. opportunities for Fall 2027, especially in groups working on reasoning, autonomous driving, embodied AI, and multimodal understanding.
